Product Requirements Document
Healthcare Doctors Adaptive Healthcare Operating System
1. Product Summary

We are building a mobile-first, web-mirrored healthcare business operating platform for Thoshi Medicals Pty Ltd trading as Healthcare Doctors.

The product is not just a CRM. It is a unified operating system that manages:

human and non-human onboarding,
contracts and consent,
tasks and tickets,
payroll and payments,
rosters and leave,
inventory and barcode scanning,
AI receptionist and AI agents,
compliance and audit,
patient-facing and staff-facing workflows,
super admin monitoring and reporting.

The central concept is:

Every important business activity creates a ticket, every ticket is traceable, and every user or system action can be monitored, approved, audited, and reported.

2. Why We Are Building It

Healthcare Doctors needs a single platform to run daily healthcare operations more efficiently, safely, and transparently.

The current business vision requires one system where:

staff can work from mobile phones,
managers can approve tasks and payments,
the business can track completed work,
payroll can be linked to verified activity,
inventory can be controlled with barcode scanning,
AI agents can support operations,
legal contracts and consents are recorded,
super admins can monitor the entire business in real time.

The goal is to reduce manual work, improve accountability, support future business growth, and create a scalable healthcare operations platform for the next 5 years.

3. Target Audience and User Roles
Primary Administrative Users

Super Admin
Owns the full system view. Can monitor all users, tickets, payroll, inventory, contracts, AI activity, compliance, reporting, and business performance.

Clinic Manager / Practice Manager
Manages day-to-day clinic operations, rosters, approvals, staff performance, patient flow, and escalations.

HR / Payroll Admin
Manages onboarding, employment documents, leave, attendance, timesheets, payroll approval, appraisals, and exit workflows.

Finance / Accounts User
Manages invoices, supplier payments, payroll payment approval, tax reporting, BAS/GST reporting, expenses, and financial controls.

Compliance Officer
Monitors training, incidents, audit trails, policy acknowledgements, privacy requirements, consent records, and compliance risks.

Healthcare Operations Users

Receptionist
Uses the mobile app for daily tasks including patient communication, appointment support, stock scanning, ticket creation, roster viewing, leave requests, training, contracts, shift handover, incident reporting, and payroll visibility.

Doctors / Practitioners
Manage appointments, consultations, clinical tasks, prescriptions, referrals, patient communications, work summaries, timesheets, and payments where relevant.

Nurses / Clinical Assistants
Manage patient support workflows, treatment room tasks, stock usage, incident reporting, equipment checks, and clinical task tickets.

Pharmacist / Medication Manager
Manages medication stock, expiry checks, prescription-related workflows, controlled item tracking, supplier issues, and barcode-based stock movements.

Inventory / Procurement User
Manages stock, suppliers, purchase orders, delivery scanning, reorder alerts, damaged goods, expiry items, and asset tracking.

External and Non-Human Actors

Patients
Interact through booking, reminders, consent, communication, check-in, feedback, and possibly telehealth workflows.

Suppliers / Vendors
Can be onboarded, contracted, linked to purchase orders, deliveries, invoices, compliance checks, and payment workflows.

Contractors / Service Providers
Can be onboarded, assigned jobs, submit completion evidence, and be paid after approval.

AI Receptionist
Can answer calls, create appointment tickets, generate follow-up tasks, escalate issues, and support patient communication under supervision.

Other AI Agents
May support finance, inventory, compliance, HR, procurement, reporting, and knowledge assistance.

Assets / Inventory / Devices / Branches
Non-human entities that can be onboarded, tracked, assigned, scanned, audited, and linked to tickets.

4. Main Business Goals
Create one central operating platform for Healthcare Doctors instead of disconnected tools.
Make every business activity traceable through a universal ticket system.
Improve payroll accuracy by linking pay approvals to verified work, attendance, rosters, and completed tickets.
Improve operational control through super admin dashboards, reports, audit logs, and user performance visibility.
Reduce manual administration using guided workflows, AI assistance, barcode scanning, and approvals.
Support healthcare compliance through contracts, consent, training, audit history, incident reporting, and privacy controls.
Improve stock and procurement control using barcode scanning, expiry tracking, delivery scanning, reorder alerts, and purchase tickets.
Enable mobile-first working so staff can perform daily activities from their phones, with mirrored web access.
Prepare for future scale across multiple clinics, branches, service categories, AI agents, and business units.

8. Assumptions
Healthcare Doctors wants one central platform rather than separate systems for CRM, HR, payroll, inventory, ticketing, and compliance.
All users will primarily access the system from mobile phones, with web access for admin and reporting.
Every operational activity should be traceable through a ticket or linked record.
Payroll approval should depend on verified attendance, rostered hours, and completed/approved work.
AI agents will assist but should not replace human approval for sensitive actions.
Barcode and QR scanning will be important for inventory, assets, tickets, forms, and identification.
Contracts, privacy acknowledgements, and training records are required for compliance.
Super admin needs full visibility across users, tasks, payroll, inventory, compliance, and AI activity.
9. Risks and Open Questions
Product Risks
The product scope is very large and may become difficult to launch if everything is built at once.
Too many features may overwhelm staff unless the user experience is simple.
Staff may resist using the system if workflows are slower than current manual processes.
AI-generated actions may create legal or operational risk if not reviewed properly.
Payroll-linked task tracking may create disputes if completion rules are unclear.
Barcode workflows may fail if physical labelling and scanning procedures are not well managed.
Healthcare compliance requirements may vary depending on services offered.
Patient data handling requires strict privacy and access controls.
Poor data quality could reduce trust in reports and AI suggestions.
Open Questions
Which user roles are required for the first release?
Which workflows must be included in the MVP?
Which payroll rules apply to employees, contractors, doctors, and suppliers?
Which contracts are legally required for each role?
Which patient-facing features are required in the first version?
Should patients log in to the same platform or a separate patient portal?
Which accounting, payroll, banking, or medical systems must be connected?
What approval limits should apply to payments, stock orders, and payroll?
Who can approve appraisal feedback?
Who can override AI-created tickets or AI recommendations?
What reports are required for tax, compliance, and management?
How many branches or locations must be supported initially?
10. Missing Information Requiring Clarification

Before product design is finalised, the team needs clarity on:

MVP scope
Which modules must launch first: onboarding, ticketing, payroll, inventory, AI receptionist, contracts, training, or super admin dashboard?
User role hierarchy
Exact permissions for super admin, manager, receptionist, doctor, nurse, finance, HR, supplier, contractor, and AI agents.
Payroll rules
How different users are paid: hourly, salary, session-based, contractor invoice, commission, or supplier payment.
Contract templates
Which legal agreements must be used for each user type and entity type.
Healthcare compliance requirements
Which specific Australian healthcare obligations apply to the services Healthcare Doctors provides.
Patient data boundaries
What patient information will be stored, who can access it, and how consent will be captured.
Inventory categories
Whether the system will manage general stock only, medicines, controlled substances, equipment, assets, or all of these.
Barcode standards
What needs barcode labels from day one: stock, assets, patients, appointments, staff IDs, visitors, forms, prescriptions, or tickets.
AI responsibility boundaries
Which actions AI can perform automatically and which require human approval.
Reporting priorities
Which reports are required for launch: payroll, tax, stock, user productivity, compliance, incidents, contracts, or AI activity.

track all key business activities through tickets,
allow staff to complete daily work from mobile,
allow super admin to view completed tasks by user and ticket number,
support task detail review and appraisal,
approve payroll based on verified work,
